# The Video Breakdown Skill

Hello friend, glad you're here.

Most of us study viral videos the same way. We watch one, think "that's good", and then try
to remember what made it work when we sit down to film.

> Why does that never stick?

Because we're watching for the topic, and the topic is the least copyable part of the video.

The part you can copy is underneath it. The hook type. The beat where the promise lands. The
line at 0:14 that stops people leaving. The pace. The ask at the end.

So I built a Claude skill that pulls all of that out for you.

Quick context on me…

I'm Brad, Head of AI for School of Hard Knocks, one of the biggest business brands in the
world with 26 million followers across platforms. I built this skill for my own content, and
I use it every week on my videos and on the creators I get inspiration from. This is the same
skill, not a watered-down version.

You give it a link. It watches the video frame by frame, reads the transcript, checks how far
it beat that creator's usual views, and hands you back a breakdown and a script template you
can fill in with your own topic.

---

## What you'll have when you're done

- A Claude skill that breaks down any Instagram reel or TikTok you paste in
- A beat-by-beat map of the video, with timestamps, what's said and what's on screen
- The psychology behind each beat, with the exact line that pulls it
- A fill-in-the-blanks script template for every video you break down
- A growing library of them in your Documents folder (and in Notion, if you want)

You don't need:

❌ a paid scraping subscription

❌ hours of rewatching the same reel

❌ a guess at why it worked

You need one thing:

✅ a link to a video that did better than it should have

## Install it (Don't Skip This)

You'll need **Claude Code**, in the Claude desktop app (the Code tab) or the terminal.

Open it and paste this:

```
Install the skill from https://github.com/bradford-marais/video-breakdown-skill
Read its INSTALL.md and follow it.
```

Claude does the setup. The one thing it'll ask you for is a free Scrape Creators key, which is
how it reads views, likes and transcripts. A new account comes with 100 free credits, which is
about 15 breakdowns.

*Takes about 5 minutes the first time.*

**Do this now:** paste that in before you close this page. That's where the progress actually happens.

---

## Use it (Don't Skip This)

Find a video in your niche that blew up. The best ones did 3x or more what that creator
usually gets, because that's proof the video won and not just the account.

Then paste it in:

```
Break down this video: https://www.instagram.com/reel/...
```

Or go after a whole creator:

```
Break down @handle's top 5 videos
```

Each breakdown saves to **Documents → Video Breakdowns**.

Each one takes a couple of minutes, because Claude actually watches the whole video.

**Do this now:** break down one video you've been wanting to copy. Then ask Claude to fill the
template in with your topic.

---

## One last thing… (The elephant in the room)

Let's be real…

You're probably wondering:

> "If I copy the structure of someone else's video, isn't that just copying?"

Fair.

I'd be asking the same thing if I were in your shoes.

So here's the truth: the template keeps the shape and throws away the content. Your topic,
your story, your proof and your voice go in the brackets. That's what every good creator
already does in their head. This just does it on paper, so you can see it.

It also won't make a weak idea go viral. It shows you how winning videos are built, and the
idea still has to be worth watching.

From guessing → knowing.

From "that's good" → "here's why".
