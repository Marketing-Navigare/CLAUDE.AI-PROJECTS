# Hook types and visual labels

Section 11 and 12 vocabulary. Use these exact strings so breakdowns line up with each other
and you can sort your library by them later.

## Hook types

Pick the one that opens the loop in the first sentence, not the one that matches the topic.

| Type | Opens the loop by… | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Prediction | saying something is about to change because of something new | "This is going to change how every agency hires" |
| Experiment | trying something live and making the viewer wait for the result | "I let AI run my calendar for a week" |
| How-to | promising a method or tool that fixes a pain point | "If your reels stall at 300 views, do this" |
| Secret | revealing something hidden that insiders know | "There's a setting nobody uses that doubles reach" |
| Contrarian | stating a belief most of the audience disagrees with | "Posting every day is killing your account" |
| Comparison | putting options side by side and holding back the winner | "I tested 4 editing apps. Only one is worth it." |
| Question | asking something the viewer already wants answered | "Why do some reels blow up with no followers?" |
| Story | dropping the viewer into one specific moment | "A client called me at 11pm last Tuesday…" |
| POV / Skit | setting up an acted scene or role-play | "POV: your client asks for 'one small change'" |

**Scroll-stop layer.** A word, sound, movement or "look at this" that lands before the hook
proper. It sits on top of one of the types above. Record both: `How-to + scroll-stop`.

## Hook delivery

`Spoken` (said, no text card) · `Overlay` (text card only, no speech) · `Both`

## Visual (what's on screen)

`Creator talking to camera` · `Creator in B-roll` · `Subject B-roll` · `Object B-roll` ·
`Screen recording` · `Screenshots` · `Example clips` · `Arrows / highlights` ·
`Animated graphics` · `Hand on screen` (a finger tracing a phone or monitor) ·
`Prop to lens` (an object held up to the camera)

## Movement

`Static` · `Slow zoom` · `Punch-in` (a quick zoom on a cut) · `Crash zoom` · `Quick cuts` ·
`Swipe transitions` · `Whip pan` (camera swung in the shot) · `Walk and talk`

## Layout

`Full-screen creator (studio)` · `Full-screen creator (out in the world)` ·
`Full-screen creator + text overlay` · `Full-screen B-roll` · `B-roll with creator inset` ·
`Split screen` · `Green screen`

## Format (one per video)

`Talking Head` · `Silent Text Overlay` · `Voiceover B-Roll` · `Vlog` · `Skit` ·
`Demo / Screen` · `Listicle` · `Rant` · `Whiteboard` · `Ranking` · `Green Screen` ·
`React / Duet` · `Other`

If a frame shows something none of these cover, describe it plainly in parentheses after the
nearest label, like `Split screen (before/after)`.
